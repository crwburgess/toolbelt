# Toolbelt: free AI prompts

Free prompts for Claude and other AI assistants, rewritten from agents I use in my own business. No sign up, nothing to install. Copy one, paste it into your assistant, and it tells you what it needs.

Every prompt is built the same way. It explains what it's doing and why it needs what it asks for, asks a couple of quick questions up front, then does the work without you having to do anything else. It shows where every finding comes from and checks it did what was asked.

I'm [Chris Burgess](https://crwburgess.com/?ref=github), a fractional CPO for engineering-led companies. You can browse these with setups for different roles on [my toolbelt page](https://crwburgess.com/toolbelt/?ref=github).

## The prompts

### Making sense of what people tell you

| Prompt | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| [Theme finder](prompts/theme-finder.md) | Find what keeps coming up in your own material, how often, and where the evidence is. |
| [Evidence finder](prompts/evidence-finder.md) | See what you can honestly claim from your transcripts, with the quotes to back up every point. |
| [Scope clarifier](prompts/scope-clarifier.md) | Understand what a client really needs, and get them to confirm it before you scope the work. |
| [Interview builder](prompts/interview-builder.md) | Write interview questions that learn from people's real experience, not your hopes for the answer. |
| [Interview answer mapper](prompts/interview-answer-mapper.md) | Sort what people said back under the questions you meant to ask, with the gaps left visible. |

### Deciding what to build

| Prompt | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| [Readiness check](prompts/readiness-check.md) | Find out how much real evidence sits behind an idea before you spend time building it. |
| [Competitor comparison](prompts/competitor-comparison.md) | Compare your product with a competitor feature by feature, with sources, and an honest note of what you still need to find out. |
| [Priority sense check](prompts/priority-sense-check.md) | Check whether the scoring you use to rank work actually does what you think it does. |
| [Decision unblocker](prompts/decision-unblocker.md) | Find the decisions hiding in a plan, and exactly what you need to learn to make them. |
| [Success mapper](prompts/success-mapper.md) | See what everyone involved actually needs from the work, so the whole job succeeds and not just each task. |

### Getting your story right

| Prompt | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| [Problem mapper](prompts/problem-mapper.md) | Work back from what you've built to the problems it solves, and tell your story from there. |
| [Deck fluff finder](prompts/deck-fluff-finder.md) | Get to what a pitch deck actually says, and see which slides are only there to impress. |
| [Claim checker](prompts/claim-checker.md) | Find the lines on your website that promise more than your product does, before a prospect does. |
| [Human editor](prompts/human-editor.md) | Take out the habits that make writing sound like an assistant wrote it, and keep your own words. |
| [Answer bank](prompts/answer-bank.md) | Stop starting every application from a blank page, without carrying old claims into the next one. |

### Finding customers

| Prompt | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| [Lead finder](prompts/lead-finder.md) | Find companies in the news that fit what you offer, and who to contact at each. |
| [Outreach writer](prompts/outreach-writer.md) | Draft a short, personal email and LinkedIn note for each lead, built around why the timing suits them. |

## Using them

Most work best in an assistant with code execution turned on, such as Claude, so counts and dates are calculated rather than guessed. Each prompt says what it needs before it starts.
